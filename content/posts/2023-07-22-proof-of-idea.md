#### I have many ideas every day

most of them are probably trash… But I decided to finally write them down so that when I realized I didn’t decide to build a billion dollar idea I will be able to say: I thought about it first!

#### But you cannot trust my website

because I can change the date and the content of every post. The same is true for every post on Twitter and Facebook, actually is even worse because they can censor everything you write on them.

#### Smart contracts

are a special piece of software that allow developers to create processes in which potentially no one has full control on them. A proof of idea could simply be a smart contract in which everyone can create a signature through a keccak256 (an hashing algorithm) of the idea’s text and then store it with the user identity.
